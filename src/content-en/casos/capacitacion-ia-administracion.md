## Problem

I proposed the course so the department would bring AI into its daily work, with an orderly method and good judgment about what data can be shared.

## What was built

I designed a 20-session course for the administration team, which starts with the most basic material and plans advanced sessions:

- **AI in everyday tools:** Excel, Word, PowerPoint and email, to save time. This is what has already been taught.
- **AI applied to accounting:** checking the consistency of records, building tables, viewing billing by month and producing indicators. In preparation.
- **Security:** what cybersecurity is, what data is not shared and how to turn off training on your own data in AI tools. In preparation.
- **Planned, for the advanced sessions:** connecting AI with other tools, automating data entry, Python with AI, local AI and the basics of machine learning.
- **A practice kit with fictitious data**, to practice without exposing real information.

## Status and result

I started teaching it in the last quarter of 2026. I teach it to the administration team, at times the work allows. The administration team already uses AI to build or review spreadsheets, formulas and calculations.
